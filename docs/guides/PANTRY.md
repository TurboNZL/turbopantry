# Pantry and shopping

The pantry is what is in the house, with amounts. The shopping list is shared and live: when anyone adds or ticks something, every phone shows it at once.

## The pantry
- **Add things by typing.** "milk", "6 eggs" or "2kg flour" (the amount is read from what you type). Tap **+** to add. Names are shown in Title Case.
- **− and +** change an amount by the item's own step (a block of butter by 250 g, eggs by 1). The number in the middle is a box: tap it and type, for the times typing is quicker.
- **Out:** the box-with-a-cross button marks something as out in one tap. The row turns into **Undo**, whose outline draws itself all the way round over 10 seconds: that is how long you have to take it back.
- **The cart button** puts the item on the shopping list. If you already have plenty (more than two steps of it) it asks first: **I'm getting more**, **I'm out of it** (the pantry goes to none now) or **Don't change the count** (buying it leaves the pantry alone).
- **Press and hold** (phone) or **right-click** (computer) any row for a menu: add to the list, mark as out, edit, more settings, delete. The same works on shopping lines and recipe cards.
- Tap a name for its few settings:
  - **How is it counted?** A switch: **Amounts** (eggs, tins), **Weight in chunks** (chicken bought in 500 g packs: each tap is one chunk, and you set the chunk size) or **Weight** (flour: small steps, type the exact number).
  - **Keep at least:** at or below this the item shows under **Running low**.
- **More settings** opens Settings → **Items**: search every item on the left, change it on the right. There you find the unit, the step and default amount, the **shop group**, where it is kept, **Also written as** (other wordings recipes use) and **Grams in a cup**. The name is shown in Title Case; **View real capitalisation** shows it exactly as it was typed.

## The shopping list
The list is the pantry's other half: **every line is a pantry item.** Type a name nobody has used before and it is added to the list *and* made a pantry item, so the two never drift apart.
- **Type to find or add.** Typing "ch" finds Chicken Breast. Put an amount first and it is used: "750 ch" or "750 g ch" asks for 750 g of chicken. A bare number is counted in the item's own unit. Suggestions show how much is in the pantry.
- **Pantry button:** scroll everything you keep (with how much is in the house) and tap **+** to add it. The search box above still filters it.
- **Cross off by tapping the line.** What you bought is added to the pantry and the line is gone: there is no "completed" list. A toast offers **Undo** if you tapped the wrong one. The pencil on the right edits the amount, note, or removes the line.
- **− and +** work on the list too, for things where the amount matters (chicken, mince). Tap the number to type one.
- **Running low** items from the pantry appear above the list, one tap to add.
- **Shop groups.** Items are grouped precisely (Fruit, Vegetables, Cheese, Pasta & noodles, Frozen veg & fruit…), guessed from the name and changeable per item. Group order follows the store you pick.
- **Stores** (Settings → Stores): make a layout per shop, then drag the groups (or use the arrows) into the order you walk the shop. Pick it on the shopping page and the list follows it. A new store starts as a copy of the one you are looking at.

## Recipes and the pantry
- **Linking.** Each recipe ingredient can be linked to a pantry item. When you import or save a recipe, a line that is the same words as an item (or as a wording you taught it) is linked by itself. Anything that could be an item you already have is shown for you to check; the rest become new items. **The recipe always keeps its own wording.**
- **The kind of ingredient matters.** "Fettuccine pasta" is not linked to "Pasta": the page offers "Pasta (less specific)" and you choose. Linking "Coles Australian Free Range Eggs" to "Eggs" teaches Eggs that wording, so it links by itself next time. Edit the wordings under the item's **Also written as**.
- Each ingredient shows **have it**, **have 3 of 5** or **none**, from the pantry. Cups, ounces and grams are compared properly (a cup of flour needs 125 g). Where the units cannot be compared (a cup of something you count in tins) it just shows what you have.
- **Add N to the list** adds only what the recipe is missing: enough in stock is left off, and when you have some but not enough only the difference is added. Things it cannot judge are added whole and named in the message so you can check.
- **I cooked this** proposes taking what the recipe used out of the pantry. **Untick anything you did not use, or change the amount**, then confirm. It can be undone, and afterwards anything now running low is offered for the list.
- Servings scale everything: change the servings first, then add or cook.

## Units
On a recipe, the units box has three modes:
1. **As written** (the default).
2. **Metric + cups:** ounces, pounds, fluid ounces and pints become grams, kilos, millilitres and litres; cups, tablespoons and teaspoons stay.
3. **Metric + grams:** as above, and a cup (a quarter cup or more) of flour, sugar, butter or rolled oats becomes grams; spoons stay, as do cups of foods without a known weight.

A cup is the 250 ml metric cup and a tablespoon is 15 ml (New Zealand and Australia). Cup weights used: plain flour 125 g, caster sugar 220 g, butter 250 g, rolled oats 90 g. Other foods are left in cups unless you set "Grams in a cup" on the item.

## Several phones
Everyone signed in sees the same pantry, list and recipes, live. If a phone loses signal, it keeps showing what it last had and catches up when it is back.
