# Pantry and shopping

The pantry is what is in the house, with amounts. The shopping list is shared and live: when anyone adds or ticks something, every phone shows it at once.

## The pantry
- **Add things by typing.** "milk", "6 eggs" or "2kg flour" (the amount is read from what you type). Tap **+** to add.
- **− and +** change an amount by the item's own step (a block of butter by 250 g, eggs by 1). Tap an item's name to change that and more:
  - **Counted in:** each, g, kg, ml, l, cups, cloves, cans…
  - **Each tap changes it by** and **Add this much by default:** tweak them per item (eggs by 6, flour by 500 g).
  - **Keep at least:** at or below this the item shows under **Running low** and can go on the list in one tap.
  - **Always have it:** for salt and oil. They are never added to the list.
  - **Also written as:** other wordings recipes use, so they link to this item (see below).
  - **Grams in a cup:** optional; lets cups of this food be shown and compared in grams.

## The shopping list
- **Type to add.** Suggestions carry the item's default amount: one tap adds it. Asking for something already on the list adds to that line instead of making a second one.
- **Tick it off** when you buy it: it goes into the pantry right away, in the amount on the list (a toast offers **Undo**; unticking takes it back out). Ticked lines wait under **In the trolley** until you press **Clear**.
- **Running low** items from the pantry appear above the list, one tap to add.
- Lines are grouped by aisle (Produce, Meat & fish, Dairy & eggs, Bakery, Pantry, Frozen, Drinks, Household, Other), guessed from the name and changeable in the item's settings.

## Recipes and the pantry
- **Linking.** Each recipe ingredient can be linked to a pantry item. When you import or save a recipe, a line that is the same words as an item (or as a wording you taught it) is linked by itself. Anything that could be an item you already have is shown for you to check; the rest become new items. **The recipe always keeps its own wording.**
- **The kind of ingredient matters.** "Fettuccine pasta" is not linked to "Pasta": the page offers "Pasta (less specific)" and you choose. Linking "Coles Australian Free Range Eggs" to "Eggs" teaches Eggs that wording, so it links by itself next time. Edit the wordings under the item's **Also written as**.
- Each ingredient shows **have it**, **have 3 of 5** or **none**, from the pantry. Cups, ounces and grams are compared properly (a cup of flour needs 125 g). Where the units cannot be compared (a cup of something you count in tins) it just shows what you have.
- **Add N to the list** adds only what the recipe is missing: enough in stock and "always have it" items are left off, and when you have some but not enough only the difference is added. Things it cannot judge are added whole and named in the message so you can check.
- **I cooked this** proposes taking what the recipe used out of the pantry. **Untick anything you did not use, or change the amount**, then confirm. It can be undone, and afterwards anything now running low is offered for the list.
- Servings scale everything: change the servings first, then add or cook.

## Units
On a recipe, the units box has three modes:
1. **As written** (the default).
2. **Metric + cups:** ounces, pounds, fluid ounces and pints become grams, kilos, millilitres and litres; cups, tablespoons and teaspoons stay.
3. **Metric + grams:** as above, and a cup (a quarter cup or more) of flour, sugar, butter or rolled oats becomes grams; spoons stay, as do cups of foods without a known weight.

A cup is the 250 ml metric cup and a tablespoon is 15 ml (New Zealand and Australia). Cup weights used: plain flour 125 g, caster sugar 220 g, butter 250 g, rolled oats 90 g. Other foods are left in cups unless you set "Grams in a cup" on the item.

## Several phones
Everyone signed in sees the same pantry, list and recipes, live. If a phone loses signal, it keeps showing what it last had and catches up when it is back.
